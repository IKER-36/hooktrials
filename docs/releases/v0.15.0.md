# HookTrials v0.15.0

## Delivery Control Plane

- Pause outbound Protect deliveries without rejecting or losing inbound provider events.
- Keep paused deliveries queued with their event ID and full attempt history.
- Resume delivery safely from Route control and let the worker continue the configured retry policy.
- Show the delivery state clearly in the existing event and Operations evidence.

The setting is additive and defaults to normal delivery. Trial and Observe routes keep their
existing behaviour.
