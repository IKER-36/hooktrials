# HookTrials v0.33.6

## Unified operational timeline

Operations now gives you a single chronological view of the latest reliability signals without
adding another destination to the dashboard.

### Added

- A new **What changed recently** timeline brings incidents, delivery recovery and alert delivery
  evidence together.
- Filters let you focus on incidents, the recovery queue or alert deliveries.
- Each item opens the relevant operational section so you can continue investigating immediately.

### Improved

- Operational activity is easier to scan on desktop and mobile with consistent status, context,
  timestamp and next-action placement.
- Quiet workspaces now explain what evidence will appear as activity arrives.
- Audit evidence remains part of Operations instead of appearing as a separate dashboard tab.

No database migration or user action is required.
